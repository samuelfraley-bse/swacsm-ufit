## 4. Sampling rate

- Per bout, (samples-1)/duration: median 95.7 Hz, IQR 92.4-98.0, 1st-99th pct 74.5-100.9, range 55.8-101.6.
- Per participant median: 77.6-98.2 Hz.
- ~35% of rows share a timestamp with the previous row (BLE bursts). These are distinct samples and are kept; we assume even spacing within a bout and resample to 30 Hz (see `STATUS.md`).

## 5. Feasibility of the team design

Training uses **day 1** only; testing uses **later days** only.

- Participants with >=2 recording days: **26**.
- Of those, participants with every class on day 1 and on a later day (needed for k = 1 set/exercise): **26**.
- Of those, participants with >=2 day-1 sets of every class (needed for k = 2): **23**.
- Day-1 sessions among the eligible: 1: 2, 2: 24.
- Classes recurring across days, among the eligible: 15: 26.
- Later-day test bouts per eligible participant: median 60 (range 30-90).
- Not eligible (only one recording day): Subject105, Subject132, Subject232, Subject274, Subject303, Subject504, Subject711, Subject912, Subject946.

## 6. Recommended grid

- **Pool: the 23 participants who support k = 2**, used for both k = 1 and k = 2 so the comparison is paired (same teams, same test athletes). Dropped from the pool: Subject267, Subject602, Subject843 (fewer than 2 day-1 sets of some exercise).
- Each repeat holds out 6 pool participants as never-recorded "new athletes"; the team is drawn from the remaining 17.
- **Team size N: 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 17.**
- **Sets per exercise per athlete, k: 1, 2** (day 1 holds two circuits). For k = 1, one of the two day-1 sets per exercise is chosen at random with a fixed seed.
- Test sets: later-day bouts of the team ("recorded") and of the held-out athletes ("new"). Test bouts are never used for training.
- Participants are the statistical unit: metrics are computed per test athlete, then summarized across athletes and repeats.
